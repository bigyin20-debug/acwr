Simulatie
λ acuut = 2/(7+1) = 0.25 | λ chronisch = 2/(28+1) ≈ 0.0667
EWMA vandaag = Load × λ + EWMA gisteren × (1−λ)
ACWR = Acute EWMA / Chronische EWMA

<0.8 = onderbelasting | 0.8–1.3 = veilige zone | >1.3 = verhoogd risico
